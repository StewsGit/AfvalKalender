---
name: retro
description: Captures a compact session retrospective and proposes evidence-based improvements to Claude instructions, skills, agents, hooks, and test workflow.
---
1. Append to `docs/retrospectives/YYYY-MM-DD.md`:
   - goal and outcome;
   - what accelerated work;
   - friction/failures and evidence;
   - escaped or nearly escaped defect;
   - one experiment for next session.
2. Delegate workflow analysis to `process-coach`.
3. Present at most three proposed workflow patches. Apply only clear, broadly reusable, low-risk improvements; otherwise leave them as proposals.
4. Keep each retrospective under 250 words.
5. Update `docs/session-log.md` with links and metrics: subtasks, commits, test retries, reviewer findings.
